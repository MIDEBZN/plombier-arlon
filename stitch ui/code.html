Complete Plumbing Solutions Built for Durability

Emergency Plumbing Response Protocols

Our dispatch control center is staffed live 24 hours a day. The moment you call, a certified coordinator provides critical interim guidance—such as locating your property's main stopcock to immediately cut pressurized water flow—while our closest GPS-tracked service van is immediately deployed to your address.

CCTV Drain Inspection & Hydro-Jetting

We eliminate guesswork using flexible fiber-optic camera snakes that transmit live 4K color feeds inside sewer lines. We pinpoint cracks, root intrusions, and grease calcifications. Our truck-mounted 4,000-PSI hydro-jetters scour pipe walls clean without digging trenches or damaging property.

Advanced Electronic Leak Detection

Never permit invasive wall smashing before an exact leak source is identified. Our specialists deploy electro-acoustic listening mics and thermal infrared imaging to detect the acoustic signature of microscopic pipe perforations beneath concrete foundation slabs and tiled walls.

Sanitary Fixture Replacements & Upgrades

Whether retrofitting water-conserving dual-flush toilet systems or installing luxury brass rainfall shower valves, our certified master technicians ensure seamless seal integrity, compliant waste drops, correct venting, and perfect silicone waterproofing that withstands decades of daily use.

Water Heater Efficiency & Maintenance

Hard water mineral accumulation drastically shortens water heater tank lifespan and drives up electricity or gas utility bills. We carry out thorough magnesium anode rod replacements, heating element tune-ups, pressure relief valve tests, and energy-efficient on-demand tankless conversions.

Bathroom & Kitchen Pipe Overhauls

Aging homes with brittle galvanized iron or lead piping experience chronic leaks, discolored water, and restricted pressure. We perform seamless whole-house repiping with multi-layer PEX tubing, guaranteeing corrosion immunity, zero joint degradation, and full municipal code compliance.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Plumbing Questions

Do you provide true 24/7 emergency plumbing services? expand_more

Yes, absolutely. We do not use third-party call centers. Our dispatch staff is active 24/7/365, including holidays and weekends. On-call plumbers are dispatched immediately for active flooding, burst pipes, gas smell concerns, and clogged main lines.

How quickly can a plumber arrive at my property? expand_more

For emergencies, our average arrival time across the metro area is between 30 and 60 minutes. When you place a call, our dispatcher tracks the closest mobile van to provide you an exact ETA and instructs you on interim shut-off steps.

How much does an emergency plumber cost? expand_more

We follow a transparent flat-rate pricing system. Upon arriving and inspecting the issue, our technician presents a complete written quote before beginning any physical work. You will never face surprise hidden fees or inflated overtime charges.

Do you guarantee your repair work? expand_more

Yes. Every repair, fixture replacement, and repiping carried out by Apex Plumbing is backed by a 100% satisfaction guarantee and a comprehensive 12-month parts and labor warranty.

Can you clear blocked drains without digging up the yard? expand_more

Yes. Over 95% of sewer and drain blockages are resolved without excavation using our advanced hydro-jetters and mechanized electro-mechanical snakes via existing inspection cleanouts.

Do you repair and replace water heaters? expand_more

Yes. We service both gas and electric storage tank models as well as modern on-demand tankless units from all top brands (Rheem, Bradford White, Rinnai, Navien). Same-day replacement is available.
